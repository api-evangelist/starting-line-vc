For much of the past several decades, our economy has been built on “productivity gains,” enabled by the significant growth in the service economy. Since 1980, the number of Americans performing service labor jobs has grown by 25% to represent nearly three quarters of all jobs in America. These service jobs are important because they enable other workers to outsource elements of their lives, which in turn provide more time for the pursuit of productivity and prosperity across all workers.
